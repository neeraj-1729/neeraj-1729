I study proton–proton collisions recorded by the CMS detector at the Large Hadron Collider, and the phenomenology of particle production in heavy-ion collisions. I work with [Dr. Amal Sarkar](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1403364) at the School of Physical Sciences, IIT Mandi.

## 🔭 What I'm working on

- **Higgs boson pair production (HH → multilepton).** Searching for HH in final states with several leptons, targeting H → WW, ZZ and ττ decays. The analysis lives in [hhmultilepton2](https://github.com/HEP-KBFI/hhmultilepton2) and is built on [columnflow](https://github.com/columnflow/columnflow), a columnar Python analysis framework.
- **Tau leptons at CMS.** Trigger scale factors for hadronically decaying taus ([Tau-Trigger-SF](https://github.com/neeraj-1729/Tau-Trigger-SF)).
- **Heavy-ion phenomenology.** Strangeness production, freeze-out and charged-hadron distributions from RHIC to LHC energies, comparing transport and statistical models with data.
